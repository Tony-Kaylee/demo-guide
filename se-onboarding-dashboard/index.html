Ramp Timeline

Four-week path from product map to certified demo.

The ramp can compress, but the gates should remain because they prove demo readiness.

Week 1

Product Map and Narrative

Understand what Rev.io PSA is, how the modules fit together, and how to tell the connected business story.

  • Map the left navigation
  • Review the current 14-step demo guide
  • Explain quote-to-cash without clicking
  • Practice a 10-minute verbal walkthrough
Week 2

Core Demo Execution

Learn the click path, transitions, Revii moments, and safe-demo guardrails.

  • Run the full demo twice
  • Record a compressed 15-minute version
  • Use tested Revii prompts
  • Avoid destructive billing actions
Week 3

Deep Dives and Branching

Build enough depth to pivot based on discovery without losing the main story.

  • Practice each feature set
  • Learn current limits and release notes
  • Connect features to buyer pain
  • Speak for 10 minutes on a chosen branch
Week 4

Scenarios and Certification

Turn product knowledge into buyer-specific judgment and final demo readiness.

  • Run vertical scenarios
  • Handle objections
  • Write an RFP-style answer
  • Pass final capstone

Buyer Scenarios

Same product story, different emphasis.

The scenario tracks help the SE pick the right branch for the room.

Telecom / UCaaS

Lead with hierarchy, billing context, recurring revenue, usage, CPE inventory, and dispatch.

  • Multiple bill profiles and service addresses
  • Metered usage and rate decks
  • Recurring services and invoice cycles
  • Service volume and revenue analytics

MSP

Lead with customer health, service execution, SLA, integrations, time, profitability, and reporting.

  • Tickets, macros, recurring tickets
  • RMM and documentation integrations
  • Time tracking and job costing
  • Executive service dashboards

Security Integrator

Lead with quote-to-install, hardware, RMR, inventory, projects, and customer visibility.

  • Installation plus monitoring quotes
  • Serialized equipment and truck stock
  • Project installs and milestones
  • Portal access for tickets and invoices

AV / Field Services

Lead with large quotes, product/labor detail, projects, purchasing, dispatch, and field completion.

  • Large multi-area product quotes
  • Project phases and Gantt
  • Reservations and POs
  • Field work through billing handoff
Revii Moments

Use AI where it improves speed or credibility.

Revii should punctuate the demo. It should not become the whole demo.

Best Moments

  • Customer activity summary
  • Open ticket and recent ticket analysis
  • Create ticket from natural language
  • Add internal activity note
  • Ask analytics questions

Practice Prompts

  • Summarize the last 90 days of activity
  • Create a service ticket for a customer
  • Add an internal note to ticket ID
  • Show tickets by severity
  • Find customers with most open tickets

Guardrails

  • Use tested prompts in live demos
  • Do not overuse AI
  • Use normal workflows when clearer
  • Review sensitive data before sharing
